Engagement patterns across a semester

When do different learner types study , and how does that change over time? This chart shows average weekly session frequency for each profile. The pattern is more revealing than total volume.

Toggle profiles on and off to compare. Notice how surface learners start strong then fade, while anxious strivers ramp up sharply before deadlines.

Average number of unique study days per week · KU Leuven VLE dataset · weeks 1–13

Key finding: High SRL students maintain steady engagement all semester. Surface learners show early enthusiasm then a steady decline , a pattern consistent with extrinsic motivation fading without internal regulation strategies.

Visualization 2

Resource use strategy , the navigation fingerprint

Total clicks tell you how much a student studied. Resource mix tells you how they studied. This is the core finding: each profile has a distinctive navigation fingerprint that maps directly onto SRL theory.

Proportion of clicks by resource type and profile

Each row is a learner profile · each column is a resource type · darker = higher proportion of total clicks

The quiz signal: Anxious strivers spend 35% of clicks on quizzes vs 4% for disengaged students. But this isn't strategic retrieval practice , it's anxiety-driven self-testing. Self-determination theory predicts exactly this: fear-based motivation (external regulation) produces repetitive checking behavior rather than deep processing.

Visualization 3

Psychological profile , what PISA data tells us

Behavioral patterns alone don't explain why students navigate differently. Psychological construct data from PISA 2022 fills that gap , showing the motivational and metacognitive profile behind each behavioral type.

SRL psychological constructs by learner profile

Scores (0–100) on six PISA-derived constructs · higher = stronger presence of that construct

Test anxiety separates profiles most clearly. Anxious strivers score 88/100 on test anxiety vs 30/100 for High SRL students. Self-efficacy and metacognitive planning together best distinguish High SRL from surface learners , both groups have moderate motivation, but only High SRL students believe they can succeed and plan accordingly.

Key findings

What the data shows

📊

Navigation strategy predicts outcomes better than volume

How students distribute clicks across resource types is a stronger predictor of SRL profile than total clicks. A student with 200 quiz-heavy clicks looks nothing like one with 200 document-heavy clicks.

F1 prediction accuracy: 0.89 · Random Forest

⚡

Anxious strivers work hard but inefficiently

Despite high session frequency and quiz engagement, anxious strivers show lower self-efficacy than High SRL students. Effort without metacognitive monitoring produces diminishing returns.

Test anxiety Cohen's d = 1.77 vs High SRL

🔍

Disengaged students reveal themselves early

Disengaged students show low diversity (5.9 unique resource types vs 9.8 for High SRL) and heavily favor announcements , checking what's due rather than engaging with content.

Active span Cohen's d = 3.07 vs High SRL

🌍

Profiles are culturally robust

PISA 2022 data across 80+ countries shows similar SRL construct distributions, suggesting these four behavioral profiles reflect universal learning regulation patterns rather than cultural artifacts.
