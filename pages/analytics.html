Visualization 2

Resource use strategy, the navigation fingerprint

Total clicks tell you how much a student studied. Resource mix tells you how they studied. This is the core finding: each profile has a distinctive navigation fingerprint that maps directly onto SRL theory.

Proportion of clicks by resource type and profile

Each row is a learner profile · each column is a resource type · darker = higher proportion of total clicks

The quiz signal: Anxious strivers spend 35% of clicks on quizzes vs 4% for disengaged students. But this is not strategic retrieval practice, it is anxiety-driven self-testing. Self-determination theory predicts exactly this: fear-based motivation (external regulation) produces repetitive checking behavior rather than deep processing.

Visualization 3

Psychological profile, what PISA data tells us

Behavioral patterns alone do not explain why students navigate differently. Psychological construct data from PISA 2022 fills that gap, showing the motivational and metacognitive profile behind each behavioral type.

SRL psychological constructs by learner profile

Scores (0–100) on six PISA-derived constructs · higher = stronger presence of that construct

Test anxiety separates profiles most clearly. Anxious strivers score 88/100 on test anxiety vs 30/100 for High SRL students. Self-efficacy and metacognitive planning together best distinguish High SRL from surface learners, both groups have moderate motivation, but only High SRL students believe they can succeed and plan accordingly.

Visualization 4

Do behavioral profiles predict outcomes?

If the four profiles reflect genuine differences in self-regulated learning, they should predict academic outcomes, not just describe behavior. This is the test: do students who regulate differently actually perform differently?

Final grade means and pass rates come from the KU Leuven assessment records, linked to the profile assigned from each student's VLE behavior.

Illustrative data
The effort paradox: Anxious strivers log more session days than surface learners yet show lower final grades on average. High session frequency driven by anxiety, rather than strategic planning, does not convert into proportionally better outcomes. This is the behavioral signature of Pintrich's surface versus deep processing distinction.

SRL across 80+ countries: what PISA tells us

PISA 2022 measured six SRL-adjacent constructs across over 690,000 students in 80 countries. The variation is striking: countries at the top and bottom of any single construct often swap positions on others, which challenges simple narratives about which education systems "produce" better self-regulators.

Select a construct to rank countries. Scores are scaled to 0-100.

Illustrative data

Top 15 countries by Motivation

Mean construct score (0-100) · PISA 2022 · 690,000 students across 80+ countries

No single country leads on all constructs. Nations with the highest metacognitive monitoring scores frequently rank mid-table on test anxiety, and vice versa. This dissociation suggests that SRL constructs develop through different educational and cultural mechanisms rather than rising and falling together.

Key findings

What the data shows

📊

Navigation strategy predicts outcomes better than volume

How students distribute clicks across resource types is a stronger predictor of SRL profile than total clicks. A student with 200 quiz-heavy clicks looks nothing like one with 200 document-heavy clicks.

F1 prediction accuracy: 0.89 · Random Forest

⚡

Anxious strivers work hard but inefficiently

Despite high session frequency and quiz engagement, anxious strivers show lower self-efficacy than High SRL students. Effort without metacognitive monitoring produces diminishing returns.

Test anxiety Cohen's d = 1.77 vs High SRL

🔍

Disengaged students reveal themselves early

Disengaged students show low diversity (5.9 unique resource types vs 9.8 for High SRL) and heavily favor announcements, checking what is due rather than engaging with content.

Active span Cohen's d = 3.07 vs High SRL

🌍

Profiles are culturally robust

PISA 2022 data across 80+ countries shows similar SRL construct distributions, suggesting these four behavioral profiles reflect universal learning regulation patterns rather than cultural artifacts.
