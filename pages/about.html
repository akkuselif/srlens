Research focus

What I study

My research sits at the intersection of educational psychology, learning analytics, and artificial intelligence. I am particularly interested in how students regulate their own learning, what behavioral signals reveal about psychological states, and how AI systems can be designed to support rather than undermine self-regulation.

🔄

Self-regulated learning

How students plan, monitor, and reflect on their own cognitive processes using Zimmerman's cyclical model as a theoretical foundation.

🧠

Metacognition

The relationship between metacognitive knowledge, monitoring accuracy, and learning outcomes across different task types and contexts.

🤖

AI in education

How large language models and adaptive systems interact with student self-regulation, and how to design AI tools that build rather than bypass learner agency.

Why SRLens

The gap this site exists to close

I built SRLens to explore the questions I work on every day as a researcher. How students learn, what motivates them, and what the data actually shows. Everything here is grounded in real research, visualized in a way that makes it easier to think through together.

This is not a finished product or a definitive guide. It is a growing collection of ideas, data, and tools built at the intersection of educational psychology and data science. You are welcome to explore, question, and learn alongside it.
