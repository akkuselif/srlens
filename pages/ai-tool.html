SRL profile assessment

The four learner profiles

Based on Zimmerman's SRL model and self-determination theory. The assessment identifies which profile best describes your natural learning approach.

High SRL

Strategic, balanced, intrinsically motivated. Plans ahead and monitors progress.

Surface learner

Passive content consumer. Avoids self-testing, relies on reading and re-reading.

Anxious striver

High effort, high anxiety. Over-tests as anxiety management rather than strategy.

Disengaged

Minimal engagement. Checks deadlines but rarely interacts with content deeply.
